Planly privacy policy

Last updated 6 October 2026

About Planly

Planly is a small, private household planner: tasks, chores, lists, projects and a budget, shared between the members of a household. It is not a commercial service. It has no adverts and no tracking or analytics, and your data is never sold.

What Planly stores

  • Your account: your email address, the display name you choose, and how you sign in.
  • What you create: tasks, chores, checklists, lists, projects, budget entries and household settings. Things you mark as shared are visible to the other members of your household; everything else is visible only to you.
  • Calendar links you add: if you subscribe to a calendar feed (an iCalendar link), the link and its events are stored privately for your account only.
  • Notifications: if you turn them on, the address your phone's push service gives Planly, so it can send reminders.
  • On your phone only: settings such as theme and haptics, and the patterns Planly uses for suggestions. These stay in your phone's storage and are not uploaded.

Planly's data is stored with Supabase, which hosts the database and server functions. Access is restricted so that each person can only reach their own data and what their household shares.

Google sign-in

If you sign in with Google, Planly receives your name, email address and profile picture from Google. They are used only to create and identify your Planly account.

Google Calendar

Connecting Google Calendar is optional. If you connect it, Planly asks for permission to manage events on calendars you own (calendar.events.owned). Planly uses it only to:

  • add a task you mark “Add to Google Calendar” as an event in your Planly calendar;
  • update that event when you change the task, and remove it when you delete the task.

Planly does not read, copy or store your other calendar events.

To stay connected, Google gives Planly a long-lived access key. It is stored only on Planly's server, never on your phone, and is used only to get short-term access for the actions above.

Planly's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is not sold, not used for advertising, not shared with anyone else, and not used to train AI models.

Removing access and deleting data

  • Google Calendar: in Planly, go to Settings › Calendars › Disconnect. This deletes the stored key and revokes it with Google. You can also remove Planly at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Notifications: turn them off in Settings › Notifications.
  • Your account and data: ask the Planly owner and everything linked to your account will be deleted. Deleting an account also deletes its stored Google key.

Contact

For questions about this policy or to request deletion, contact the Planly owner using the support email shown on Planly's Google sign-in screen.

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too.
