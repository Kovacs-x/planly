Manual two-account matrix: A→B assigned: B can complete/reopen, cannot edit/delete. A→Anyone: B view-only. A→A: B view-only. Reassign B→A while B is open: B loses completion authority after reconciliation. Household→Private: B loses task. Remove B from Household: B loses visibility/authority. Complete on B while A is open: A updates through Realtime. Reopen on B while A is open: A updates again.
